# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project's issues, pull requests and discussions to be a
harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer at
the email address listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/godwire).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a
response deemed necessary and appropriate, which may include removal of
comments, issues or pull requests, or a temporary or permanent ban from the
project's spaces.
